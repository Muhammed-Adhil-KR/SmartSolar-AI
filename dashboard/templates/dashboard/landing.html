{# ---------------- System types ---------------- #}
Works with your setup

On-grid, off-grid or hybrid

The advice adapts to the kind of system you have.

On-grid

Connected to the electricity grid. Advice covers when to use solar power directly and when exporting or importing makes sense.

Off-grid

Runs on solar and a battery. Advice protects your essential appliances and your battery reserve.

Hybrid

Solar, battery and grid together. Advice balances all three to use as little grid power as possible.

About & Contact

About this project

SmartSolar is an Intelligent Decision Support System for Solar Energy, built as an academic mini project. It combines a machine-learning solar forecast with a rule-based advisor to help households use solar energy better.
